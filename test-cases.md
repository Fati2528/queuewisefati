QueueWise - Test Cases

Test Case 1: Queue 1 is Faster

Input Values:

- People ahead in Queue 1: 2
- Service time in Queue 1: 2 minutes
- People ahead in Queue 2: 5
- Service time in Queue 2: 3 minutes

Expected Result:

- Queue 1 waiting time: 4 minutes
- Queue 2 waiting time: 15 minutes
- Queue 1 is faster.

Test Case 2: Queue 2 is Faster

Input Values:

- People ahead in Queue 1: 5
- Service time in Queue 1: 3 minutes
- People ahead in Queue 2: 2
- Service time in Queue 2: 2 minutes

Expected Result:

- Queue 1 waiting time: 15 minutes
- Queue 2 waiting time: 4 minutes
- Queue 2 is faster.

Test Case 3: Both Queues Have Equal Waiting Time

Input Values:

- People ahead in Queue 1: 2
- Service time in Queue 1: 3 minutes
- People ahead in Queue 2: 3
- Service time in Queue 2: 2 minutes

Expected Result:

- Queue 1 waiting time: 6 minutes
- Queue 2 waiting time: 6 minutes
- Both queues have equal waiting time.
